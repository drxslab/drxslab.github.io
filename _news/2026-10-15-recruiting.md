The lab is newly founded, and we are looking for our first members.

Open positions span postdoctoral researchers, PhD and master's students, undergraduate
researchers, research assistants, and visiting scholars. Full details — what each role
involves, what we look for, and how to apply — are on the [Join Us]({{ '/join/' | relative_url }}) page.

A note on what "newly founded" means for you. You would get more of the PI's time, more
say in what gets worked on, and genuine ownership of research directions. You would also
get less established infrastructure and fewer senior students to learn from. That is a
real trade-off, and it is worth weighing honestly before you write.

If the four [research directions]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) interest you, please get in touch.
