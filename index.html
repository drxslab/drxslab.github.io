Complexity, Safety, Socialization 任务难度，安全交互，社会化融合

The lab is led by Wenqiang Xu, Assistant Professor at the Institute of Trustworthy Embodied AI, whose research centers on the safety of robot interaction.

We study how robots can work in physical contact with people and objects without putting them at risk — spanning human motion mechanisms, omni-modal perception and high-frequency force control, safe interaction policies, and the design of novel embodiments and sensors.

Research Directions 研究方向

Four directions, one question: how can robots act in physical contact with the world without putting it at risk?

Human Motion Mechanisms 人体运动机理

Musculoskeletal modeling and biomechanically grounded motion understanding.

Omni-modal Perception & Force Control 全模态感知与高频力控

Fusing vision, touch, force, and proprioception into one picture of contact.

Safe Interaction Policy 安全交互策略

Policies for navigation, locomotion, and manipulation that stay safe under contact.

Novel Embodiments & Sensors 新型本体与传感器

Making safe physical interaction a property of the hardware itself.
